# Choirs & Legions

**Choirs & Legions** lets you play Agents bound to celestial, infernal, or other spiritual Powers and hierarchies.

The Setting can be played as a secret war in the modern world, a far more visible struggle, a historical chronicle, or a campaign spanning several eras.

## Powers

Powers may be **Archangels**, **Demon Princes**, spiritual sovereigns, founding figures, or other beings capable of commanding, creating, or transforming Agents.

They may belong to vast hierarchies, act almost alone, or maintain complex relationships with their peers.

A Power is not necessarily defined by a simple opposition between Good and Evil. Depending on the world, Choirs and Legions may defend incompatible principles, pursue different goals, or be divided by conflicts of their own.

## Agents

Agents may be Angels, Demons, incarnated beings, servants shaped by their Power, chosen Humans, or creatures whose nature depends on the framework.

Their bond with their Power may take very different forms: obedience, loyalty, debt, lineage, admiration, fear, mission, or rebellion.

Their nature also gives them particular possibilities.

:::note[Example]

An Angel with wings can spread them and fly. A Demon able to sense lies can recognize when someone is lying to them.

These abilities do not automatically require a roll. They first define what is possible for the Agent. They become Bets when they genuinely matter in a resolution.

:::

## Situations

A campaign may involve, among other things:

- a mission entrusted by a Power;
- contradictory orders coming from different levels of a hierarchy;
- protecting, corrupting, or recruiting a Human;
- conflict between Agents of rival Powers;
- discovering that a faction is pursuing a different goal from the one it claims;
- disobedience, schism, or an attempt at emancipation;
- consequences of deeds committed centuries earlier.

**Choirs & Legions** may also be combined with other Settings: an Archangel may have opinions about Vampires, ancient gods, or the new Powers of the modern world without any of them belonging to the Archangel's own hierarchy.
