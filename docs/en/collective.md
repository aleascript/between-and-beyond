# The Collective

> We made it. No one commands it.

The Collective is the Horizon of what humans have made together and what now exceeds them: Money, the Nation, the City, Communication. No one wanted it as it is; millions of gestures produced it, and it now acts on each of us.

It is the most recent Horizon in appearance, and the oldest in reality: the Romans already honored the genius of every city. Old gods sometimes fade when no one honors them anymore; new ones are born from our habits.

## Why Agents

**The difference of scale.** The Collective is made of millions of people, yet none of them can grasp it. It has no body, no face, no hand. To act somewhere, it needs someone to embody it: the one who sets a price, who carries a flag, who speaks to millions.

**What each side experiences.** Through Agents, humans experience being carried — or crushed — by something greater than themselves, which they nonetheless made. Through them, the Collective meets what it does not contain: a face, an exception, a conscience, a refusal.

**What is transformed.** Here, the loop is shortest. The Powers of the Collective live only on what humans do: when practices change, they change — or die.

## Powers

### Money

What makes everything comparable to everything else.

- *Everything can be exchanged*
- *Circulates endlessly*
- *Measures everything*
- *Exists only if people believe in it*

### The Nation

A people who think of themselves as a single body.

- *Us and the others*
- *Tells itself an origin*
- *Demands the dead*
- *Outlasts its children*

### Communication

What makes messages circulate — and the messages themselves.

- *Connects everything it touches*
- *Amplifies*
- *Is never silent*
- *Does not tell true from false*

### The City

Strangers living together.

- *Gathers strangers*
- *Never sleeps*
- *Rebuilds itself on itself*
- *Forgets its inhabitants*

## Families of Agents

### Changers — Money

Lenders, merchants, bankers, those who set prices. The Bond is a contract.

*Knows the price of everything* · *Their signature binds* · *Money comes to them*

**What the figure lets us think:** what has a price and what does not.

### Heralds — the Nation

Soldiers, national poets, founders, those who carry the flag. The Bond is belonging.

*Speaks in the name of all* · *Their presence rallies* · *Does not back down*

**What the figure lets us think:** belonging; dying for something one has never seen whole.

### Voices — Communication

Town criers, journalists, influencers, whistleblowers. The Bond is an audience.

*Heard by all* · *Knows what is circulating* · *Their words spread*

**What the figure lets us think:** being heard by everyone and being no one; speech without a body.

### Genii Loci — the City

The spirit of a neighborhood, a street, a station. The Bond is a place.

*Knows every street* · *Cannot leave the city* · *The city helps them*

**What the figure lets us think:** dwelling; a place that remembers those who lived there.

## What appears

- coinage, then paper money, then credit;
- civil registries, identity cards, passports, borders;
- printing, the telegraph, radio, a telephone in every pocket;
- the subway, street lighting, the city that no longer sleeps;
- currencies without banks;
- the gift, which never stopped existing alongside exchange.

## An Agent

:::note[Example — Samir, changer of Money]

- **Bond keywords:** *Knows the price of everything*, *Their signature binds*.
- **Acquired keywords:** *Grew up in a family that gave everything away*, *Volunteers at a community pantry*.

Money believes that *everything can be exchanged*. Samir knows that some things are not given back. If he gets the gift recognized as belonging to Money, his Power will finally know what has no price — and other changers will learn to give.

:::

## Situations

**Bringing a novelty.** A Voice discovers village gossip, far older than itself; a Genius Loci discovers remote work, and a city emptying out.

**Contending for a novelty.** Currencies without banks appear. Money wants them; Communication claims them, since they are nothing but messages; the Nation fears them, since they ignore borders. Their Agents contend over what they will become.

**Transforming one's own Power.** The Genius of a working-class neighborhood watches the City drive out those who brought it to life. They want their Power to stop *forgetting its inhabitants*.

## Tones and time

The Collective lends itself to satire, thriller, social drama, and political fable.

It also sheds light on the other Horizons: an ancient god of the Divine may discover that Communication does better than it does, and a Power of the Collective that ancient rites were already technologies.
