# Signals

**Signals** explores encounters with Powers that come from elsewhere — or are simply impossible to understand through ordinary Human categories.

The Setting can range from relatively grounded science fiction to something far stranger.

## Powers

Powers may be extraterrestrial civilizations, collective intelligences, distributed consciousnesses, autonomous probes, cosmic phenomena, or entities whose true nature remains unknown.

Some communicate clearly. Others manifest only through **signals**, dreams, anomalies, patterns, biological transformations, or effects that cannot be interpreted with certainty.

A Power does not need to be humanoid, or even to think like an individual.

## Agents

Agents may be contacted Humans, hybrids, altered beings, artificial creations, organisms designed for a mission, interpreters, or people able to perceive what others cannot.

Some know exactly whom they serve. Others possess only part of the message.

Their abilities may come from physical transformation, technology, an implant, transmitted knowledge, or a change that is harder to explain.

## Situations

A campaign may involve, among other things:

- receiving a signal nobody else can understand;
- a mission whose true purpose remains unknown;
- recovering an artifact or organism;
- several extraterrestrial Powers pursuing incompatible goals;
- Human Agents interpreting the same message differently;
- a gradual transformation caused by contact;
- discovering that a long-observed phenomenon was a manifestation of a Power;
- the question of whether something is actually trying to communicate.

:::note[Example]

An Agent can perceive certain transmissions within ordinary radio noise. That ability makes listening possible.

When they try to isolate a message amid interference, jamming, and limited time, the same ability may become a Bet in the resolution.

:::

**Signals** can naturally cross over with other Settings. An extraterrestrial entity may be mistaken for a god, a demon, or a spirit without the game having to decide immediately who is right.
