# Blood & Night

**Blood & Night** features Powers tied to blood, lineages, predation, transformation, and creatures of the night.

Vampires and Werewolves are the most obvious examples, but the Setting can include many other families of creatures and Powers.

## Powers

A Power may be a very ancient Vampire, the founder of a bloodline, an entity of Blood, an ancestor of a pack, a primordial creature, or a force from which several monsters draw their nature.

Not every Power needs to be an individual. A bloodline, a pack, or an ancient curse may also be carried by a Power, depending on the world.

The exact place of a creature therefore depends on the Setting.

:::note[Example]

In one world, a Vampire may be an Agent created by a Power of Blood.

In another, a Vampire several thousand years old may itself be a Power surrounded by descendants, familiars, and Agents.

The common rules do not impose a single answer.

:::

## Agents

Agents may be Vampires, Werewolves, descendants, familiars, hunters bound to a Power, heirs to bloodlines, or beings transformed by Blood or Night.

Their keywords describe what their nature allows them to do: see in darkness, take animal form, survive certain injuries, sense blood, command a pack, or exert a particular influence.

These possibilities may also have limits: sunlight, hunger, territory, oath, lunar cycle, dependence on a bloodline, or any other constraint specific to the framework.

## Situations

A campaign may involve, among other things:

- conflict between bloodlines or packs;
- protecting a territory;
- a debt owed to an ancestor or creator;
- the disappearance of an ancient Power;
- conflict between an Agent's nature and what they want to preserve of their Humanity;
- a hunt led by Humans or other Agents;
- an uncontrolled transformation;
- the appearance of a new bloodline;
- an alliance against a threat that does not come from the Night.

**Blood & Night** does not need to be exclusively horrific. Depending on the chosen tone, the same Setting may be tragic, violent, political, romantic, epic, or even lighter.
