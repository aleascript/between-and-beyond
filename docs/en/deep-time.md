# Time

> The mountains were here before us. The stars will be here after.

Time is the Horizon of what came before us and will outlive us: geological ages, destiny, the return of the seasons, the moment that does not come back. A human life sees only a spark of it.

Every age has seen it differently: a thread spun by the Moirai or the Norns, a wheel turning without end, an arrow pointing toward progress, a clock, a probability. And every age has invented figures to inhabit what no life can inhabit: oracles, immortals, the reincarnated, gamblers whom luck favors.

## Why Agents

**The difference of scale.** A human life lasts a few decades; Time is counted in ages. Eternity does not fit in a moment, and destiny can only be seen from very far away. There must be beings who overflow a single life — through duration, through vision, through return.

**What each side experiences.** Through Agents, humans touch what exceeds their lives: knowing the future, seeing the past, lasting. Through them, the Powers of Time meet what they ignore: urgency, regret, promises, what one does with a life *because* it is short.

**What is transformed.** An immortal who has loved mortals too much is no longer indifferent to duration. A Power that welcomes what they bring back changes what it means to last.

## Powers

### Destiny

What is already spun: the Moirai, the Norns, *maktub*.

- *Everything is already spun*
- *No one escapes it*
- *Reveals itself in fragments*
- *Never goes back*

### The Moment

The Greek *kairos*: the opportune moment, luck, the chance that does not come twice.

- *Happens only once*
- *Gives no warning*
- *Favors the bold*
- *Remembers nothing*

### The Cycle

What returns: the seasons, the ages of the world, the wheel of rebirth.

- *Everything returns*
- *Every end is a beginning*
- *Nothing is lost*
- *Knows nothing of progress*

### Duration

What lasts: stones, stars, what was here before and will be here after.

- *Was here before, will be here after*
- *Slow*
- *Wears everything down*
- *Indifferent to the moment*

:::design[Design note]

*Chance & Destiny*: the Moment and Destiny are the two faces of Time most readily played together. Their Agents can contend over almost anything that happens.

:::

## Families of Agents

### Oracles — Destiny

Sibyls, seers, diviners. The Bond is a vision.

*Sees what will be* · *Cannot not see* · *Never quite believed*

**What the figure lets us think:** knowing without being able to change; what freedom remains when everything is written.

### Gamblers — the Moment

Adventurers, players, those whom luck favors as long as they dare. The Bond is a wager kept.

*Senses the opportunity* · *Luck smiles on them* · *Cannot refuse a bet*

**What the figure lets us think:** the moment that does not come back; what one does with one's luck.

### The Reborn — the Cycle

Those who return, life after life, and remember. The Bond is the wheel itself.

*Remembers past lives* · *Always returns* · *Recognizes those they have known*

**What the figure lets us think:** being several lives; what persists of oneself when everything changes.

### Immortals — Duration

Those who do not die of old age and watch the centuries pass. The Bond is a duration received.

*Does not age* · *Has seen everything* · *Outlives everyone they love*

**What the figure lets us think:** watching everything one loves die; memory without end; what a life that never ends is worth.

## What appears

- the calendar, the mechanical clock, standard time;
- history as a discipline, archives;
- probability, and with it insurance: chance given a price;
- geology, which discovers deep time;
- carbon dating, which makes bones speak;
- photography, which stops the moment;
- messages left for future centuries.

## An Agent

:::note[Example — Cassandra, oracle of Destiny]

- **Bond keywords:** *Sees what will be*, *Cannot not see*.
- **Acquired keywords:** *Insurance actuary*, *Raising her son alone*.

Destiny *reveals itself in fragments*, and Cassandra has never been able to say *when*. At work, she has learned to speak in probabilities. If she gets probability recognized as belonging to Destiny, oracles will be able to say *how likely* — and Destiny will stop being entirely certain.

:::

## Situations

**Bringing a novelty.** An immortal discovers photography, and that faces can last; one of the reborn discovers archives, and finds their previous life in them.

**Contending for a novelty.** Probability appears. Destiny sees in it the future finally quantified; the Moment sees chance put in a cage; the Cycle sees the same events returning. Their Agents contend over what the calculation of chances will become.

**Transforming one's own Power.** An oracle has seen her son's death. She wants Destiny to be able to leave one thread free — and to do so, must undo one of its Attributes: *Everything is already spun*.

## Tones and time

Time lends itself to melancholy, epic, philosophical tale, and adventure.

It is the Horizon where campaigns across the ages find their most natural form: a campaign's [timescale](horizons.md#timescales) becomes the very subject of play.
