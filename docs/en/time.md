# Time

A **Metaxy** campaign can stay within one period or cross years, centuries or millennia.

Immortal or long-lived Agents can make historical change part of play, but this is an option rather than a requirement.

Every age has its own horizon: death, the divine, or the unknown are not seen the same way from one century to the next. A campaign that crosses the ages shows what a Horizon becomes — and what the Agents have made of their Powers.

This page will later describe ways to choose and use that temporal scale.
