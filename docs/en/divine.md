# The Divine

> What is absolute cannot touch what is particular without ceasing to be absolute.

The Divine is what founds, commands, or judges: the origin, the law, the sacred, what is worth more than anything else. Every age has given it other faces — thunder on the mountain, a throne surrounded by choirs, a heaven that grants or withdraws its mandate, an inner voice, an emptiness we no longer know how to name.

*Angel* comes from the Greek *angelos*: messenger. No figure is more exactly an Agent.

## Why Agents

**The difference of scale.** The Divine is eternal, whole, without exception. The human world is made of nothing but exceptions: cases, circumstances, persons. A perfect law does not know what a particular case is. Someone has to carry it that far.

**What each side experiences.** Through Agents, humans meet the call, the law, grace, temptation — something that asks more of them than themselves. Through them, the Divine meets what it does not contain: freedom, doubt, pity, the choice not to obey.

**What is transformed.** An angel who has lived among humans learns what a particular life costs. A Power that welcomes what they bring back may learn mercy — or the fall.

## Powers

### The Sword

An Archangel: protection and judgment. It guards what is entrusted to it and cuts what must be cut.

- *Protects what is entrusted to it*
- *Judges without appeal*
- *Cannot lie*
- *Obeys the Throne*

### The Abyss

A Demon Prince: the adversary, the tempter, the one who said no.

- *Tempts, does not compel*
- *Always offers a choice*
- *Knows everyone's desire*
- *Creates nothing*

### The Thunder

A god of an ancient pantheon — one of those who strike, protect their own, and quarrel with their peers.

- *Strikes*
- *Protects its own*
- *Demands offerings*
- *Quarrelsome among equals*

### Heaven

An impersonal order of the world, which does not speak but grants and withdraws its favor.

- *Everything has its place*
- *Grants and withdraws the mandate*
- *Does not speak*
- *Punishes disorder with disorder*

## Families of Agents

The suggested traits are **Bond** traits: each comes from the relationship itself or from the Attribute shown. The Agent's Anchors belong to them and remain to be written.

### Angels — the Sword

Created to serve. The Bond is creation.

- *Wings of light*, from the creation itself;
- *Voice of the Archangel*, from *Judges without appeal*;
- *Senses the presence of evil*, from *Protects what is entrusted to it*.

**What the figure lets us think:** can one carry a message without being changed by it? Obedience, and the freedom it presupposes.

### Demons — the Abyss

Fallen, rebellious, or born of shadow. The Bond is allegiance.

- *Senses lies*, from *Knows everyone's desire*;
- *Reads desire*, from *Knows everyone's desire*;
- *Offers a pact*, from *Always offers a choice*.

**What the figure lets us think:** temptation; the price of what we desire; the freedom to say no, even to what exceeds us.

### Children of the Gods — the Thunder

Demigods, heroes, descendants of a union between a god and a mortal. The Bond is kinship.

- *Superhuman strength*, from *Strikes*;
- *The god's blood speaks in them*, from the kinship itself;
- *Cannot refuse a challenge*, from *Quarrelsome among equals*.

**What the figure lets us think:** being half; inheriting what exceeds us; what we owe to parents greater than ourselves.

### Prophets — any Power of the Divine

Humans who have been called. The Bond is a call they did not choose.

*Hears the voice* · *Sees what is coming* · *Their words unsettle those who hear them*, which come from the call itself. Other traits depend on the Power that calls.

**What the figure lets us think:** being the human voice of what is not human; the border between revelation and madness.

## What appears

- writing, and with it sacred texts;
- printing, which puts those texts in every hand;
- forgiveness made into an institution: amnesty, truth commissions;
- human rights, a sacred without a god;
- secularism, freedom of conscience, atheism;
- psychiatry, which treats those who hear voices.

## An Agent

:::note[Example — Azriel, angel of the Sword]

- **Bond:** creation. Traits: *Wings of light*, *Voice of the Archangel*.
- **Anchor** *Court-appointed defense lawyer*: *Pleads for those in the wrong*, *Knows the courthouse corridors*.
- **Anchor** *Loves a woman who believes in nothing*: no trait written yet.

The Sword *judges without appeal*. Azriel has spent years defending the guilty. If he gets the defense — pleading for the one who is wrong — recognized as belonging to the Sword, *Pleads for those in the wrong* will pass into the Bond, and the Archangel will have learned that judging first requires listening. And other angels will begin to plead.

:::

## Situations

**Bringing a novelty.** An angel discovers that a human court can forgive; a demon discovers that one can believe without desiring anything.

**Contending for a novelty.** Human rights appear: a sacred without a god. The Sword wants to protect them; the Abyss sees in them the freedom to choose; Heaven sees a new order. Their Agents contend over what this sacred will become.

**Transforming one's own Power.** A demon has learned to compose music. They want the Abyss to be able to create — and to do so, must undo one of its Attributes: *Creates nothing*.

## Tones and time

The Divine can be played as a contemporary secret war, an epic tragedy, or a satire — the celestial hierarchy lends itself readily to bureaucracy.

It joins [Authority](authority.md): Heaven grants the Crown its mandate, and Revolution takes it back.

It can also be played across the ages: an angel may have seen writing born, heretics burned, and churches empty.
