# Horizons

A **Horizon** is what exceeds us: Death, Time, the Divine, the Unknown, the Collective…

A horizon only exists from a point of view: it is always *our* horizon. We never reach it; it recedes as we advance. And every age has its own: death was not seen the same way in the age of the pyramids, of the danse macabre, or of the intensive care unit.

A Horizon is not a place. One does not travel into Death as into another world: one approaches it, through those who stand between.

## Horizon, Powers, Agents

The Horizon is the unit of play in **Metaxy**. It brings together:

- several **Powers**, the forms this Horizon has taken across ages and cultures;
- **families of Agents**, the figures through which each Power becomes present in the world;
- the **novelties** that appear in the world and that none of these Powers contains yet.

The Powers of a single Horizon may cooperate, ignore each other, or contend for what appears in the world. They never confront each other directly: their Agents do it for them.

A Horizon also specifies what makes up the **domain** of each of its Powers, what could truly extend it, and what a Power gives back to the Agent who brings it something it did not yet possess.

## Why Agents?

A Horizon does not merely supply creatures and powers. It must be able to answer three questions:

1. **What difference of scale makes its Agents necessary?**
2. **What does their appearance allow each side to experience?**
3. **How can this relationship transform each of them?**

Each family of Agents must also be able to say why it is a figure of this Horizon. The vampire does not belong to Death because it is frightening, but because it lets us occupy an impossible position: immortal without being alive.

A figure that answers none of these questions has no place on a Horizon, even if "it would be cool".

## Choosing your figure

Choosing an Agent also means choosing the figure from which you want to approach what exceeds us. During creation, each player can ask a question — of themselves, not of their character:

> **What does this figure let me think that I cannot think otherwise?**

No one needs to answer out loud. But the answer often says what the player will come to the table looking for.

## Combining

Horizons are played **à la carte**.

- A single Horizon can be explored in depth, across the ages, with Agents from different families.
- A table can ignore a family of Agents that does not speak to it.
- A campaign can bring together Powers from several Horizons.

What connects very different figures is not a shared cosmology: it is what exceeds them. The Powers do not need to share the same origin, understand one another, or be explained by a single theory.

:::note[Example]

An Agent of the Passage investigates dead who no longer pass over. They discover that a recent Power, **Communication**, keeps their voices online, and that an Angel is interested in what those voices say about the soul.

Death, the Collective, and the Divine meet here around a single novelty.

:::

## The Horizons

- [**Death**](death.md) — what no one can occupy while alive.
- [**The Divine**](divine.md) — what founds, commands, or judges.
- [**Dreams**](dreams.md) — what everyone visits every night without being able to inhabit it awake.
- [**Time**](deep-time.md) — what came before Humanity and will outlive it.
- [**The Unknown**](unknown.md) — what resists all our categories.
- [**The Collective**](collective.md) — what we have made together and what now exceeds us.

Other Horizons remain possible, provided they answer the three questions of "Why Agents?".

## Familiar figures

The figures you already know have their place. They are simply arranged by what exceeds them rather than by genre.

| If you are thinking of… | Look toward… |
| --- | --- |
| vampires, ghosts, psychopomps, grim reapers | [Death](death.md) |
| angels, demons, ancient gods, demigods, prophets | [the Divine](divine.md) |
| sandmen, nightmares, incubi, dream messengers | [Dreams](dreams.md) |
| immortals, oracles, the reincarnated, the chosen of destiny | [Time](deep-time.md) |
| extraterrestrials, hybrids, contactees, cosmic horror | [the Unknown](unknown.md) |
| new gods of money, media, and nation, spirits of cities | [the Collective](collective.md) |

The same figure can belong to several Horizons depending on what you want to do with it: the vampire is a figure of Death, but an immortal vampire crossing the centuries also looks toward Time.
